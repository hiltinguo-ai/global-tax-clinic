1. CORPORATE EXCISE TAX (Form 355)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Who owes:           All MA-incorporated C-corps and foreign corps with MA nexus
Rate:               8% of net income apportioned to MA, OR $2.60 per $1,000 of
                    tangible property/net worth — pay whichever is higher
Minimum excise:     $456
Estimated payments: Required if tax exceeds $1,000 — paid quarterly (Form 355-ES)
Return due:         March 15 (calendar year) or 15th day of 3rd month after fiscal year end
Apportionment:      Single sales factor — MA revenue / total revenue (for most industries)

Estimated tax schedule:
  Q1  due Apr 15  (25% of prior year tax, or 25% of current year estimate)
  Q2  due Jun 15  (25%)
  Q3  due Sep 15  (25%)
  Q4  due Jan 15  (25%)
  Any balance due with return by Mar 15

2. SALES & USE TAX ON SAAS (Form ST-9)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Is SaaS taxable:    YES — prewritten software delivered electronically is taxable
                    as tangible personal property (830 CMR 64H.1.3)
Rate:               6.25%
Filing frequency:   Monthly if annual liability > $1,200
                    Quarterly if $100–$1,200
                    Annually if < $100
Due date:           30th of month following end of filing period
Economic nexus:     $100,000 in MA sales in prior or current calendar year (Oct 2019)
