Accountant receives counsel's confirmed nexus list and the entity's financial data (revenue by jurisdiction, payroll, expenses, assets). For each nexus jurisdiction, the accountant computes apportionment percentages per that jurisdiction's rules, determines taxable income or taxable base, applies the correct tax rate, and calculates the total liability. Produces estimated payment schedules for each jurisdiction and prepares the data needed to populate each return. Handles book-to-tax reconciliation, identifies applicable deductions and credits, and flags any calculation edge cases for counsel review.
