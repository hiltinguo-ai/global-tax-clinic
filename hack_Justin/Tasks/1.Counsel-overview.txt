Counsel receives the entity's onboarding data (incorporation state, entity type, employee locations, revenue by jurisdiction, industry classification) and produces a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction nexus determination. For each jurisdiction where the entity has customers, employees, or property, counsel checks physical presence triggers, economic nexus thresholds, and industry-specific taxability rules. The output is a definitive list of jurisdictions where the entity owes tax, what tax types apply (corporate income/excise, franchise, sales/use, employer withholding, gross receipts), and the relevant filing forms and frequencies. Any jurisdictions marked "REVIEW" require further analysis before the accountant can proceed.
