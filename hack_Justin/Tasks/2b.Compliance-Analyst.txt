Compliance Analyst receives counsel's nexus list and the accountant's liability calculations and builds the full filing calendar with dates, forms, amounts due, and filing methods. For each obligation, the analyst confirms the correct filing portal, gathers required supporting documentation, populates the forms, and submits by the deadline. Maintains a tracker of all filings with status (pending, filed, confirmed) and monitors regulatory sources across all active jurisdictions for rate changes, new rules, form updates, or threshold adjustments that may require counsel review.
